1 Introduction

SafeRoute ("we", "us", "our") is a personal safety navigation application developed by Group 4. We provide real-time route planning, danger zone awareness, and emergency SOS capabilities to help users stay safe.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and what rights you have over it. It applies to all users of the SafeRoute mobile app (iOS and Android).

By creating an account or using the SafeRoute application, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

3.2 Location Data

SafeRoute is a location-based safety app. Processing your location data is fundamental to the core service. We collect:

  • Real-time GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude, and accuracy in metres) while you are using the navigation feature
  • Route history, the start/end points of routes you have calculated
  • Danger zone reports, coordinates associated with community-reported hazards
  • Emergency location, your precise GPS coordinates at the moment an SOS is triggered
⚠ Important — Location Data & Your Safety Your real-time location is processed exclusively to provide navigation and emergency services. Location data is never sold to third parties or used for advertising. Emergency location data is shared with emergency contacts via SMS/call through our partner RabbitMQ only when you actively trigger an SOS.

3.3 Emergency Contact Data (Third-Party PII)

When you add trusted contacts to your account, we store:

  • Contact name
  • Phone number (e.g. +353891234567)
  • Relationship label (optional, e.g. "Partner", "Parent")
  • Whether the contact is designated as primary or secondary

These individuals have not created a SafeRoute account. By adding a contact, you confirm that you have obtained their consent to receive emergency notifications from SafeRoute on your behalf, and that they are aware their contact details will be stored securely in our system.

5 Consent & How to Withdraw It

Where processing is based on consent, we will ask you to provide it clearly and separately for each purpose before any processing begins. Consent is:

  • Freely given — refusing consent does not prevent you from creating an account, though some features (e.g. navigation, SOS) will not be available without location consent
  • Specific — one consent per purpose (location tracking, emergency SMS to contacts, etc.)
  • Informed — we explain what data is collected, why, and who receives it before asking
  • Unambiguous — a clear affirmative action (checkbox tick or toggle) is required

Consent records are stored in our database with a timestamp, the version of this policy in force at the time, your platform, and the purpose for which consent was given.

5.1 How to Withdraw Consent

You may withdraw consent at any time, with no penalty. Withdrawal takes effect immediately for future processing; it does not affect the lawfulness of processing that occurred before withdrawal.

  • In-App: Navigate to Settings > Privacy > Manage Consents and toggle off any purpose
  • By Email: Send a withdrawal request to saferouteapp2025@gmail.com

6 How We Use Your Personal Data

We use the personal data we collect for the following purposes only:

  • Account creation and authentication: to register your account, verify your identity, and keep your account secure via Auth0
  • Navigation and route planning: to calculate safe pedestrian routes using your current GPS location
  • Danger zone awareness: to surface community-reported hazard data relevant to your route
  • Emergency SOS: to notify your trusted contacts of your location via SMS and phone call (via RabbitMQ) when you trigger an SOS
  • Feedback processing: to receive and respond to your in-app feedback and bug reports
  • Security and fraud prevention: to detect unauthorised access, rate-limit abusive requests, and maintain system integrity
  • Service improvement: using aggregated, anonymised usage metrics only — no individual profiling

12 Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction, in accordance with Article 32 GDPR.

12.1 Technical Measures

  • Encryption in transit: all communication between your device and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher (HTTPS)
  • Encryption at rest: mobile tokens stored in the iOS/Android Secure Enclave (hardware-backed encryption). Database fields containing PII are encrypted at the field level
  • Authentication security: JSON Web Tokens (JWT) signed with the RS256 algorithm, verified via Auth0's JWKS endpoint on every request
  • Session security: sessions have a 30-day sliding window with a 90-day absolute maximum. Revoked tokens are blacklisted in Redis
  • Rate limiting: authentication endpoints are limited to 10 requests per 60 seconds per IP. Global API rate limit of 100 requests per 60 seconds
  • Token blacklisting: invalidated tokens are blacklisted for 5 minutes post-expiry to prevent replay attacks
  • Secrets management: all API keys and credentials are stored in an encrypted secrets manager; none are embedded in application source code

12.2 Organisational Measures

  • Access control: database access follows the principle of least privilege; each microservice uses a restricted database role
  • Staff training: all team members with access to personal data receive annual GDPR and security awareness training
  • Incident response plan: a documented 72-hour breach notification procedure is in place (see Section 13)

13 Data Breach Notification

In the event of a personal data breach, we will act in accordance with Articles 33 and 34 of the GDPR:

  • We will assess the risk of the breach within 24 hours of becoming aware of it
  • Where required, we will notify the Data Protection Commission within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach
  • Where the breach is likely to result in a high risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you directly without undue delay
  • We maintain an internal breach register recording all incidents, their impact, and remediation actions taken
